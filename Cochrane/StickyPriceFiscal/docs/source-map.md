# Source map

Cochrane (2023), *The Fiscal Theory of the Price Level*, Chapter 5, "Fiscal theory in sticky-price
models" (pp. 117–186), with the Online Appendix sections it relies on: §A1.3 (pp. 561–564), §A1.7
(pp. 569–571), §§A1.9–A1.13 (pp. 573–585) and §A2, (2.1)–(2.3) (p. 595). Each formal claim is
listed with its Lean name, relative to the namespace `Cochrane.StickyPriceFiscal`. Page numbers are
book pages; "App." marks the Online Appendix, whose equations are numbered (1.21)–(1.53) and
(2.1)–(2.3). The book has no labelled propositions, so rows are keyed by equation number, figure,
table, section or page. Rows follow the book section by section, and each heading names the modules
that formalise the section: §§5.1–5.7 with the appendix sections next to the sections that use
them, the §5.5 model in a genuine probability model right after §5.5, followed by §§5.1–5.4, 5.6,
the responses, Table 5.1 and joint stationarity in a genuine probability model, §§5.7–5.7.1
with Brownian shocks after the deterministic §§5.7–5.7.1, the operator calculus after §5.7.2,
§§5.7.2–5.7.5, the frictionless limit and the integral forms of p. 163 with Brownian shocks
after the deterministic §§5.7.3–5.7.5,
and the matrix method for every matrix after App. §1.13, then the links between sections
(including continuous-time transversality with discontinuous forcing and the
discrete-to-continuous limit of the peg path), the copied Blanchard–Kahn theorem, the copied
measure-theoretic core, the Brownian and Itô tools copied from the Monetarism project, and the
shared tools.
Survey codes E19–E54 refer to `notes/survey_cochrane_ch4-5.md`; corrections are explained in
[corrections](corrections.md).

Discrete-time statements hold for an arbitrary `ExpectationSystem`, so under perfect foresight and
under genuine uncertainty. The modules `PolicyRulesMeasure`, `PolicyRulesMeasureNews` and
`PolicyRulesMeasureStationary` restate §5.5, and the modules `TidyR6MeasFiscalNK` to
`TidyR6PathwiseTransfer` restate §§5.1–5.4 and 5.6, for a measure-theoretic expectation system
(`ExpectationsMeasure.MeasExpectationSystem`, Mathlib's `condExp`), with equations almost
everywhere, so that they also hold in full-support infinite-horizon models such as the i.i.d.
economy. Continuous-time statements are about deterministic paths after a date-0
("MIT") shock, as in the book's responses, except in `TidyR7StochCTPath`, `TidyR7StochCTBrownian`
and `TidyR7StochCTUnique`, where §§5.7–5.7.1 are solved with Brownian shocks and the copied Itô
integral, and in `TidyR8StochKernel` to `TidyR8StochCTRules`, where §§5.7.2–5.7.5, the
frictionless limit and the integral forms of p. 163 are.
