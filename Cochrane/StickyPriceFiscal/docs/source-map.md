# Source map

Cochrane (2023), *The Fiscal Theory of the Price Level*, Chapter 5, "Fiscal theory in sticky-price
models" (pp. 117–186), with the Online Appendix sections it relies on: §A1.3 (pp. 561–564), §A1.7
(pp. 569–571), §§A1.9–A1.13 (pp. 573–585) and §A2, (2.1)–(2.3) (p. 595). Each formal claim is
listed with its Lean name, relative to the namespace `Cochrane.StickyPriceFiscal`. Page numbers are
book pages; "App." marks the Online Appendix, whose equations are numbered (1.21)–(1.53) and
(2.1)–(2.3). The book has no labelled propositions, so rows are keyed by equation number, figure,
table, section or page. Rows follow the book section by section, and each heading names the modules
that formalise the section: §§5.1–5.7 with the appendix sections next to the sections that use
them, then the links between sections, the copied Blanchard–Kahn theorem and the shared tools.
Survey codes E19–E54 refer to `notes/survey_cochrane_ch4-5.md`; corrections are explained in
[corrections](corrections.md).

Discrete-time statements hold for an arbitrary `ExpectationSystem`, so under perfect foresight and
under genuine uncertainty. Continuous-time statements are about deterministic paths after a date-0
("MIT") shock, as in the book's responses.
