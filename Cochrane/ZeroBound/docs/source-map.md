# Source map

Cochrane (2023), *The Fiscal Theory of the Price Level*, Chapter 20, "The zero bound"
(pp. 465–485), the model claims and arithmetic of Chapter 21, "The Covid inflation"
(pp. 486–494), and the future-selection claims of Chapter 24 (pp. 522–524). Each formal claim is
listed with its Lean name, relative to the namespace `Cochrane.ZeroBound`. Page numbers are book
pages. The book has no labelled propositions, so rows are keyed by equation number, figure,
table or page. Rows follow the book section by section, and each heading names the modules that
formalise the section: §20.1, §20.2.1 (with its stochastic orbit results and the continuous-time
limit of Figure 20.3) with the Chapter 24 claims, §§20.2.2–20.2.5, the stochastic Werning model of
§§20.2.2–20.2.5 (two sections), §20.2.6, §20.3, Chapter 21 (with the
nonlinear helicopter), then the links between the modules, the links with Galí (2015) and Woodford
(2003), Galí's commitment optimum (lift-off timing, existence and the shape of the zero-rate spell,
the zero post-shock natural rate), the common model for the bound and Woodford's Proposition 6.9,
the restated blocks (Woodford Chapter 2 global dynamics, Woodford Chapter 6 sequences and moment
constraint, the measure-theoretic expectations of Chapter 22, the Brownian, Itô and
Ornstein–Uhlenbeck tools of the Monetarism and FiscalConstraints projects) and the shared tools.

The discrete-time results (§20.1, §20.2.1, §20.2.6, Chapter 24) hold for an arbitrary
`ExpectationSystem`, so under perfect foresight and under uncertainty, except where a row says
"perfect foresight". An *equilibrium* is an adapted process satisfying the model equations at
every date and state; *determinate* means at most one bounded adapted equilibrium for every
forcing (`ExpectationSystem.Determinate`). The continuous-time results (§§20.2.2–20.2.5) are
about deterministic paths `ℝ → ℝ` that satisfy the ODEs by `HasDerivAt` off a countable set;
the equilibrium criterion is boundedness on `[0, ∞)`. Their stochastic versions (the
`TidyR7StochWerning` and TidyR8StochWerning modules) are about processes `ℝ → Ω → ℝ` on a filtered probability space
that satisfy `E_t dZ = D dt` in integrated form, with bounded moments as the criterion. Survey error codes E1–E11 and E20 are
those of the survey of Chapters 20–25 (`notes/survey_cochrane_ch20-25.md`); see
[corrections](corrections.md). Rows marked "restated" reproduce results proved in an earlier
project, with the namespace adapted.

## Lift-off timing: the bound binds throughout the shock (LiftoffTiming)

inflation then exclude a slack date in the shock. Every declaration cites Cochrane §20.2.4
