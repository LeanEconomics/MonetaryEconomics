# Source map

Cochrane (2023), *The Fiscal Theory of the Price Level*, Chapter 16, "The new-Keynesian models,
equilibrium selection" (pp. 363–396), with the precise claims of Chapter 12, "Interest-rate
targets", and Online Appendix A3, §§3.1.1–3.1.2 (pp. 613–616). Each formal claim is listed with
its Lean name, relative to the namespace `Cochrane.NewKeynesianSelection`. Page numbers are book
pages. The book has no labelled propositions, so rows are keyed by equation number, figure, the
survey's claim codes (Chapter 12 claims P1–P8, non-identification theorems NI-1 to NI-6) or page.
Rows follow the book section by section, and each heading names the modules that formalise the
section: the innovation operator and the Chapter 2 model the chapter rereads, Chapter 12 (with
the sticky-price claims P8 and P1), §§16.1–16.10 (each followed by its household-problem and
under-uncertainty sections where these exist), Online Appendix A3, then the links between modules
and to Woodford (2003), the restated Woodford blocks, the restated measure-theoretic and
household modules, and the shared tools.

The linear results hold for an arbitrary `ExpectationSystem`, so under perfect foresight and under
uncertainty; an *equilibrium* of (16.4) is an adapted inflation process satisfying (16.4) at every
date, and *determinate* means exactly one bounded equilibrium for every bounded adapted
disturbance. The nonlinear results of §16.8 and §16.10 are under perfect foresight unless the row
or its section says otherwise; the "under uncertainty" sections prove them for every
`ExpectationSystem` and almost surely on a probability space. Survey error codes E1–E6 are those of
the survey of Chapters 11–19 (`notes/survey_cochrane_ch11-19.md`); see
[corrections](corrections.md). Rows marked "restated" reproduce results proved in an earlier
project, with the namespace adapted.
