# Corrections to the source

Places where a claim in Chapter 8 or Appendix G of Woodford (2003) is false, imprecise, or relies
on an unstated hypothesis, and where the Lean statement departs from the printed one. In each
case the Lean statement proves the stated version and cites the original. Codes E14–E22 and S2 are
the Chapter 8 items of the chapter survey (`notes/survey_woodford_ch7-8.md`); E10 is a Chapter 7
item met again in §3.1. Each was confirmed or refuted by formalisation before being recorded here,
and entries are revised if formalisation shows the finding itself to be wrong. Several displays
that looked suspect, or that the book asserts without proof, turned out to be correct; they are
listed in the confirmed section. The general theory of §§1.4–1.5 (Propositions 8.1–8.4, which
the book states without proof) is proved in general, and its findings are recorded in the tables
below. A final section lists corrections to this project's own earlier hypothesis structures. These
are formalisation errors, not errors in the book. Out of scope: the derivation of the
Giannoni–Woodford criteria (2.41)–(2.45), the parity remark of p. 727, and the plotted curves of
Figures 8.1–8.6. Lean names are relative to the namespace `Woodford.OptimalPolicyRules`.
