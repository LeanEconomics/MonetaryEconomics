# Corrections to the source

Places where a claim in Chapter 4 or Appendix C of Woodford (2003) is false, imprecise, or relies
on an unstated hypothesis, and where the Lean statement departs from the printed one. In each
case the Lean statement proves the stated version and cites the original. Entries are recorded
when found and revised if formalisation shows the finding itself to be wrong. Three suspected
errors turned out to be correct: on p. 683 the Phillips curve is printed in first differences, as
the proof needs, and a level form would be a different restriction
(`ThreeByThree.level_nkpc_differs`); the thresholds of footnotes 38 and 39 are right
(`Learning.BullardMitra.taylorB00_gt_one_iff`, `Learning.BullardMitra.taylorB10_neg_iff`); and the
p. 682 bound derived from (C.11)–(C.12) holds (`ThreeByThree.caseI_impossible_46`). The
determinacy condition for the inflation-inertia model, first proved only as a sufficient
condition, is also necessary (`InflationDeterminants.inertia_determinate_iff`). Three items are not
formalised: sufficiency in Proposition 4.6, which the book itself omits, and Proposition 4.10
(Preston) are deferred to a later project on the general theory; the stochastic-approximation
convergence results of pp. 265–266, which the book cites from Evans and Honkapohja, are out of
scope, and E-stability is formalised through the eigenvalues of `DT − I`. Lean names are relative
to the namespace `Woodford.NeoWicksellianFramework`.
