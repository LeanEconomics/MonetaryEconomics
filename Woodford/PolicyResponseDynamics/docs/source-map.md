# Source map

(pp. 320–380), with the proofs in Appendix D (pp. 687–691). Each formal claim is listed with its
Lean name, relative to the namespace `Woodford.PolicyResponseDynamics`. Page numbers are book
pages. Rows follow the book: Chapter 5 §1.1–§3.4, then Appendix D.1–D.3. A claim proved in the
appendix is listed at the place where the chapter states it; the intermediate displays of each
appendix proof are listed under that appendix section. Results of Chapters 3 and 4 that Chapter 5
uses (Proposition 4.3, sufficiency in Proposition 4.4, Propositions C.1 and C.2) are restated in
this project and listed where Chapter 5 uses them.
