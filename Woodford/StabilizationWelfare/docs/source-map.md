# Source map

Woodford (2003), *Interest and Prices*, Chapter 6, "Inflation Stabilization and Welfare"
(pp. 381–463), with the proofs in Appendix E (pp. 692–708). Each formal claim is listed with its
Lean name, relative to the namespace `Woodford.StabilizationWelfare`. Page numbers are book pages.
Rows follow the book: Chapter 6 §1–§5, then Appendix E.1–E.8. A claim proved in the appendix is
listed at the place where the chapter states it; the intermediate displays of each appendix proof
are listed under that appendix section. Every welfare proposition has two rows. The **Layer A**
row is the exact identity: the Taylor polynomial of utility equals minus the quadratic loss plus
an explicit term independent of policy (t.i.p.) plus an explicit remainder of weighted degree at
least three. The **Layer B** row is the statement about actual utility: along a one-parameter
family of economies of amplitude `ε`, utility (expected, discounted where the book discounts)
equals t.i.p. minus `ε²/2` times the loss plus `O(ε³)`, and a lower loss ranks policies for all
small `ε > 0`. Results restated from earlier chapters (the Calvo slope, the habit root `δ`, the
reset-price problem) are listed where Chapter 6 uses them. The shared core (`Determinacy`,
`Expectations`, `ExpectationsFiniteTree`, `ForwardSolution`) is cited where a row uses it.
