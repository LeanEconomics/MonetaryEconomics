# Corrections to the source

Places where a claim in Chapter 6 or Appendix E of Woodford (2003) is false, imprecise, or relies
on an unstated hypothesis, and where the Lean statement departs from the printed one. In each
case the Lean statement proves the stated version and cites the original. Entries are recorded
when found and revised if formalisation shows the finding itself to be wrong. Several displays
that looked suspect turned out to be correct: the algebra of (E.2)–(E.4) and of the assembly in
App. E.1 is exact (`Prop61.thmA1`); the printed (E.27) differs from the exact Taylor polynomial
only by a t.i.p. and a term that is cubic because of the normalisation `n₁φ₁ + n₂φ₂ = 1`
(`TwoSectorWelfare.upperCES_E27`, `TwoSectorWelfare.abs_S_le`); (E.16) and the substitutions of
p. 700 hold exactly at the steady state, not merely up to `O(Δ̄)` (`Transactions.smSig_eq`,
`Transactions.chi_etaI_vbar_eq`); `V[i] = 13.83` against `3.72² = 13.8384` is rounding of the
standard deviation (`Transactions.vi_rounding`); footnote 26's "on the order of .05" is right
(`PriceDispersion.fn26_value`); the weights of Proposition 6.13, whose proof the book omits, are
confirmed (`StickyWageWelfare.prop_6_13`). The new equilibrium modules confirm, and in places
strengthen, what the chapter takes for granted: the family of equilibria behind every Layer B
statement exists and is smooth in the amplitude (`NonlinearNKMarkov.markov_equilibrium`,
`SmoothInSigma.Model.exists_smooth_solution`); under the exact timeless Ramsey policy the
first-order targeting rule `ŷ_t − ŷ_{t−1} + θπ_t = ` (decaying predetermined terms) and
`λ_x = κ/θ` hold for every distortion `Φ`, not only `Φ = 0`, because `a(λ̄₄ − λ̄₁)/Γ = θ` exactly
(`NonlinearNKMarkovRamseyLin.key_theta`, `NonlinearNKMarkovRamseyLin.commitment_system`); the
Markov representation for AR(1) shocks needs no finite-support assumption, for the Taylor rule and
for the Ramsey policy alike (`NonlinearNKMarkovAR1Markov.ar1_markov_representation`,
`NonlinearNKMarkovRamseyAR1.ramsey_ar1_markov`); the timeless Ramsey equilibrium is unique among
all bounded processes, adapted or not (`NonlinearNKMarkovRamseyNonAdapted.ramsey_unique_all`); and
Proposition 6.15's Layer B holds over any probability space of goods, including the continuum of
Calvo reset histories (`NonlinearNKMarkovGoodsLayerB.prop615_goods_layerBM`). The Rotemberg
modules confirm footnote 44 of Chapter 3: under the equivalence map the first-order solutions of
the exact Rotemberg and Calvo models agree state by state (`NonlinearRotembergLinks.first_order_agree`),
and the agreement extends to the discounted second-order costs of inflation along any bounded path
(`NonlinearRotembergWelfare.discounted_cost_equiv`), but not to total second-order welfare of
trend inflation, which differs whatever the distortion (`NonlinearRotembergTrendWelfare.trend_welfare_differ`);
the Rotemberg targeting-rule coefficient `θ_R` equals Calvo's `θ` iff `Φ = 0`
(`NonlinearRotembergRamseyLin.thR_eq_theta_iff`). The Chapter 7 claims the chapter anticipates
(the date-0 optimal commitment of p. 415, footnote 71, long-run inflation for any `Φ`) are
formalised with the precisions recorded below. Out of scope: the
numerical Figures 6.1–6.6; and the outputs of numerical optimisation behind Figure 6.1 and
Table 6.2 (the values of `λ̂` and `λ̃`, and footnote 57). Only the exact arithmetic claims of the
tables are formalised. The remaining modelling hypotheses of the Layer B (actual-utility)
statements are listed at the end. Lean names are relative to the namespace
`Woodford.StabilizationWelfare`.
