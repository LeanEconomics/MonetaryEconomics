# Source map

Woodford (2003), *Interest and Prices*, Chapter 7, "Gains from Commitment to a Policy Rule"
(pp. 464–533), with the proofs in Appendix F (pp. 709–715). Each formal claim is listed with its
Lean name, relative to the namespace `Woodford.GainsFromCommitment`. Page numbers are book pages.
Rows follow the book, section by section, with the modules that formalise each section named in
the heading: Chapter 7 §1–§5, then Appendix F.1–F.6, then the cross-links between modules and the
shared tools. A claim proved in the appendix is listed at the place where the chapter states it;
the intermediate displays of each appendix proof are listed under that appendix section. Every
statement holds for an arbitrary `ExpectationSystem`, so under perfect foresight and under
uncertainty; optimality is proved by Lagrangian sufficiency, and "unique" means unique among
bounded adapted processes. Results restated from earlier chapters (the Chapter 4 IS and
real-balance relations, the determinacy theory behind (3.9)) are listed where Chapter 7 uses them.
The shared core (`Determinacy`, `Expectations`, `ExpectationsFiniteTree`, `ForwardSolution`) is
cited where a row uses it. Dates: `CostPushCommitment` runs on `ℤ`; in `ImpulseResponses` and
`NaturalRateCommitment` Lean date 0 is the economic date `t₀ − 1`; elsewhere `t₀ = 0`.
