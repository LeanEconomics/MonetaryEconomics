# Corrections to the source

Places where a claim in Chapter 7 or Appendix F of Woodford (2003) is false, imprecise, or relies
on an unstated hypothesis, and where the Lean statement departs from the printed one. In each
case the Lean statement proves the stated version and cites the original. Codes E1–E13 and S1 are
the items of the chapter survey (`notes/survey_woodford_ch7-8.md`); each was confirmed or refuted
by formalisation before being recorded here, and entries are revised if formalisation shows the
finding itself to be wrong. Several displays that looked suspect, or that the book asserts without
proof, turned out to be correct; they are listed in the last section. Out of scope: the
`O(‖·‖²)` qualifiers of Propositions 7.1–7.5 and 7.15, which belong to the Chapter 6 welfare
approximation (only the exact linear-quadratic statements are formalised); the learnability and
E-stability claims of pp. 519, 528 and 532–533; Taylor rules with negative coefficients (fn 33);
and the numerical Figures 7.1–7.6 and the calibrated rates of fn 13. Lean names are relative to
the namespace `Woodford.GainsFromCommitment`.
